SubSense AI – Autonomous Financial Copilot

SubSense AI is an AI-powered financial copilot that automatically understands bills, subscriptions, and spending patterns to help users save money before unnecessary charges occur.

Unlike traditional expense trackers, SubSense AI combines OCR, Large Language Models (LLMs), expense forecasting, and personalized recommendations to provide proactive financial guidance.

Problem Statement

People often face:

Forgotten subscription renewals

Hidden recurring charges

Scattered bills across Gmail, PDFs, and receipts

Unexpected monthly expenses

Lack of intelligent financial insights

Overspending without realizing it

Our Solution

SubSense AI acts as a personal financial assistant that:

Reads bills from Gmail and uploaded receipts

Detects recurring subscriptions automatically

Predicts future monthly expenses

Sends renewal warnings before charges occur

Identifies duplicate or unnecessary subscriptions

Detects price increases

Provides AI-powered money-saving recommendations

Answers finance-related questions through an AI chat assistant
